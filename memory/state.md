draft, so they come back every session.

## Reminders and deadlines

The one reminder list. Every future-dated reminder or check is a row here.
Record the **effective** deadline (for example, a notice period ending) next to
the printed one, and remind on the earlier date.

| Remind on | Item | Printed date | Effective deadline | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | Example: confirm the refund arrived | (none) | YYYY-MM-DD | Waiting |

Status values: Waiting, Due, Done, Unknown, Dropped.
